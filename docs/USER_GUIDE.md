# KhanCoalSupplier user guide

## Sign in

Enter the username or email address the Owner assigned to you. On your first sign-in, replace the temporary password with one of your own. If your account uses two-factor authentication, enter the six-digit code shown by your authenticator. For first-time setup, add the key shown on screen to your authenticator before entering a code. The production workspace requires the Owner to complete this setup.

Use the menu by your name to open your profile or sign out. On your profile, you can change your password and sign out every device. Use **Forgot password?** on the sign-in page to request a reset link. Check the inbox associated with your staff account.

## Staff accounts

Open **Settings**, then **Staff accounts**. Choose **Add staff member**, enter their details, and choose the role that matches their duties. Share the temporary password privately. The staff member chooses their own password on first sign-in.

Use **Manage** beside a staff member to change their role, disable their access, or set a new temporary password. These actions end their current sessions. A company must keep at least one active Owner.

## Customers

Open **Customers** and choose **Add customer**. Enter the business name and any contact details. Set a credit limit when the customer has one. The Owner can enter an opening receivable and its effective date when the customer already owes the company money.

Use the search box and status filter to find a customer. Choose **Edit** to update their details or set their status to **Inactive**. A dispatch directory only shows active customers. The customer list shows recorded sales, receipts, and the balance through the current business day.

## Suppliers and mines

Open **Suppliers** and choose **Add supplier**. Enter the company or mine name, contact details, and a default mine when you know it. The Owner can enter an opening payable with the date the balance takes effect.

Open a supplier's record to add or edit mines. Set an active mine as default if the supplier usually dispatches from it. A supplier can have several active mines. Use a payable adjustment with a reason to correct the recorded opening balance.

## Company and bank settings

Open **Settings** to update the company name, contact details, and logo used on documents. Choose the currency label, weight label, date format, and grouping style used for amounts. Changes to an order or voucher prefix apply to future numbers.

Use **Bank and cash accounts** to add or rename a payment option. Deactivate accounts that staff should stop selecting. Previously issued records keep their original account details.

## Truck dispatches

Open **Order Entry** or use **New order** in the header. Select the dispatch date and generate the number of rows you need. Choose a customer, supplier, and mine for each truck. Enter the vehicle number, weight, and rates available to your role. Expand **Bilty and notes** to record a slip reference or instructions.

Use **Same supplier for all** and **Same mine for all** when a batch uses one source. **Copy last row down** copies the trip details into a new row with a blank bilty number. Press Tab to move between fields. Ctrl+Enter adds a row. Confirm a repeated vehicle when it is making another trip on the same date.

Choose **Add all orders** to save the batch. Correct any highlighted fields and resubmit with the same form. The Accountant or Owner can price and post the Operator's pending dispatches.

Use **Orders for selected date** to review the saved dispatches. Open **Slip PDF** for one dispatch or **Print day report** for the selected date. **Edit** opens the fields allowed by your role. **Duplicate** copies a dispatch into a new form. Owners can void a dispatch with a reason while retaining its order number and audit history.
