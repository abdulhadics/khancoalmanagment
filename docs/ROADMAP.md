# Roadmap

The first release covers one company and its coal dispatch, receivables, payables, and accounting reports. The deployment and accessibility gates are in [Delivery plan](DELIVERY_PLAN.md). The localization milestone follows release hardening and includes native-speaker review.

Potential future projects include a native dispatcher app, multi-company operation, yard stock management, payroll, accounting integrations, and SMS or WhatsApp notifications. Each needs separate scope and acceptance criteria.
