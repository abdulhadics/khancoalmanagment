# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

TypeScript, Next.js App Router, PostgreSQL, Prisma, Tailwind CSS, and shadcn/ui. Local development and deployment run natively on Windows.

## Users

A Pakistan-based coal supplier operates with one owner and a staff of two to ten. Dispatchers capture truckloads, accountants post and reconcile the transactions, and viewers consult dashboards and reports. The owner also manages access, financial periods, and backups.

## Purpose

Track coal bought from mines and suppliers and sold to industrial customers. Each truck dispatch ties together the parties, vehicle, weight, buy and sell rates, and resulting amounts. Orders feed customer receivables and supplier payables. Receipts and payments reduce those balances.

## Operating context

Dispatch entry is repetitive and keyboard-driven. Staff need bulk entry on desktop and a usable form on phones and tablets. Finance staff compare filtered reports, follow individual ledgers, and print dispatch slips and vouchers. Reports use PKR, explicit weight units, and the Asia/Karachi business date.

## Brand commitments

Use KhanCoalSupplier as the product name. The reference establishes navy and indigo, Inter, white surfaces, and a sidebar grouped by operational and reporting tasks. Use text and icons alongside colors for financial status.

## Evidence

The prototype and 33 screenshots cover dispatch entry, sales and ledgers, accounts, reports, party records, and settings. Values from those screens are reference material. Production data migration requires the client’s source export and reconciliation approval.

## Product principles

- Reconcile every report to the same postings and show the period used.
- Keep dispatch input fast and preserve entries when validation fails.
- Reveal financial information only to the roles authorized to use it.
- Make corrections and period changes traceable.

## Accessibility and inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA with keyboard navigation, visible focus, responsive tables, and text labels for statuses. Start with English and retain the structure for Urdu and Pashto and right-to-left layout.
